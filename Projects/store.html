Questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders leave our studio in 1–2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3–6 days; you receive a tracking link by email.

Can I return an item?

Yes. Contact us within 30 days of delivery and we send a prepaid label. Refunds go back to your original payment method.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, bank transfer and cash on delivery in selected areas.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, to most countries. Shipping costs and times show at checkout.
